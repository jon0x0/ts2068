NATIVE TS2068 CARTRIDGE · PLAYABLE PROTOTYPE

Mine crystals. Destroy Sinistar.

Press Space or joystick fire at the native arcade-logo title screen to begin. You have three lives and three Sinibombs. Shoot planetoids and collect their crystals: each gives three bombs, up to 24. Workers build Sinistar from twenty pieces. When he awakens with “Beware! I Live!”, dodge his pursuit and land thirteen Sinibombs to win. Bomb hits now slow and briefly stun him using the arcade rules.

Booting cartridge…

Q/A/O/P move · Space shoots · B launches Sinibombs · R restarts · F toggles fast chase mode (off initially). Joystick 1 supports directions and regular fire. Release directions to coast. Hold fire for repeated shots or B for successive bombs.

Explore the scrolling world and mine its 18 persistent planetoids. Objects move offscreen and remain in the world; shattered rocks replenish away from you. Ten stars scroll with the camera.

Top radar: white is you, cyan is planetoids, red is the worker, and yellow is Sinistar. Blue corners show the current viewport. The player stays centered on radar.

This is a functional world milestone. Precomputed assembly masks improve the controlled scrolling-overlap fixture from 8.62 to 9.81 fps. Stationary assembly remains around 19 fps; busy scrolling scenes remain slower.

Precomputed masks: all eight horizontal mask phases build in the back cache. The selected lookup is reused and assembly composition touches only dirty cells. Transparent gaps and fully covered-object culling remain enabled. New pieces appear after cache preparation completes.

F: fast mode. At most two visible planetoids, with mining supply retained before awakening. Natural fast assembly measures 8.48 fps and fast pursuit 15.04 fps. Current performance and tests. Original-source gameplay audit and remaining features.
