Sinistar controls

Fly: Q up · A down · O left · P right. Combine keys for diagonals; release to coast.

Fight: Space start / shoot · B Sinibomb · Enter start from title / attract · R restart during play. Hold Space or B to repeat.

Settings: S game sound · C rock bounce (starts on) · F fast mode (starts off; two visible rocks). Press and release to toggle.

High-score initials: O previous letter · P next letter · Enter or Space confirm each of three letters.

Touch: drag the D-pad; hold FIRE or SINIBOMB. FIRE starts the game too. Gamepad: joystick 1 directions and fire; use B or SINIBOMB for bombs.

Click or press a key to enable browser audio. Attract mode is silent. Audio On/Off mutes browser output separately from S. Fullscreen retains this guide; Esc exits.
