Sinistar

Playable port · v58

Mine crystals. Build your Sinibomb supply. Destroy Sinistar before he catches you.

Press Space to start and shoot. Move with Q up, A down, O left and P right. Launch a Sinibomb with B.

Keyboard controls

Click the game screen first if keys aren’t reaching it.

Fly

Q ↑O ←A ↓P →

Hold keys to accelerate. Combine vertical and horizontal keys for diagonals. Release them to coast.

Fight

Space
Start / shoot
B
Launch Sinibomb
R
Restart the game

Hold Space for repeat fire, or B for successive bombs. You start with three lives and three bombs.

Adjust

S
Toggle game sound
C
Toggle rock bounce
F
Toggle fast chase mode

Bounce starts on; fast mode starts off. Fast mode limits visible planetoids to two.

Entering high-score initials: use O / P to change the letter, then Enter or Space to confirm each letter. Enter also starts a game from the title.

Survive the hunt

  1. Mine. Shoot planetoids and fly over the released crystals. Each crystal adds three Sinibombs, up to 24.
  2. Watch. The worker builds Sinistar from twenty pieces. On the radar: you are white, rocks cyan, the worker red and Sinistar yellow.
  3. Fight back. When you hear “Beware! I live!”, keep moving and launch your bombs. Thirteen hits destroy Sinistar.
Sound, gamepads and emulator controls

A click or keypress enables browser audio. The attract demo is deliberately silent. During play, S controls the game’s sound; TSRun’s separate Mute switch must also be off.

A compatible gamepad on Joystick 1 supplies directions and fire. Use B for bombs. Physical gamepad behavior depends on your browser and controller.

Use TSRun’s Full button for fullscreen, Pause to pause, CRT for the display filter and Keys for its on-screen keyboard. On a phone, open Keys to reach the game’s letter keys.

A playable TS2068 adaptation; hardware validation is pending. One worker, projectile and Sinibomb are active at a time. This is not a complete reproduction of every arcade feature.
