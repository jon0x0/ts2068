Mine crystals. Destroy Sinistar.

Press Space or joystick fire at the native arcade-logo title screen to begin. You have three lives and three Sinibombs. Shoot planetoids and collect their crystals: each gives three bombs, up to 24. Workers build Sinistar from twenty pieces. When he awakens with “Beware! I Live!”, dodge his pursuit and land thirteen Sinibombs to win. Bomb hits now slow and briefly stun him using the arcade rules.
