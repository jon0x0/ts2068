Export format & provenance

ZIP includes PNG frames and sheets, bitmap bytes, row-major 8×1 attributes, transparent preserve masks, column-major paired sprite rows, your settings and source provenance. It does not modify a cartridge or disk image.

Legal 8×1 colours at this alignment do not establish safe gameplay overlaps, hitboxes, RAM placement or performance. Larger storage dimensions require integration review.
