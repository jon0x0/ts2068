Why the extra RAM?

CP/M needs writable low memory. The cartridge supplies 32KiB of DOCK RAM while the upper OS resides in HOME RAM, leaving a 52,992-byte TPA. TSPico supplies this mapping plus separate banked SRAM for RAMDisk. A suitably modified PicoCartridge/PicoROM could provide the required RAM too.

64 or 80 columns

Use COLUMNS 64 or COLUMNS 80. The older published PicoVideo V4 reports version register 10 hex, lacks the supported text renderer and captures writes to banked video-address ranges. That can look like screen corruption while CP/M is still running. PicoVideo 4.2 filters banked writes and supplies 80-column text. TSPico firmware is a separate device and upgrade.

Disk compatibility

Single-sided 160KB and double-sided 320KB standard DSK images share disk bytes through our TAP adapters. This is CP/M filesystem/image compatibility, not a general Oliger controller emulation on TSPico. A is the only implemented drive. The browser and Fuse require a cartridge matching the image geometry.

Original TSPico 1.00 has booted and run the games on hardware. Many failure cases, modern services and PicoVideo 4.2 are tested with native Z80/protocol models; see the validation notes for limits. This release is CP/M 2.2, leaving a separate path for future 3.x work.
