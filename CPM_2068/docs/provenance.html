Source and emulator provenance

Digital Research CCP/BDOS source is vendored unmodified in third_party/cpm22:

  • Repository: https://github.com/brouhaha/cpm22
  • Commit: 01018abbccce0bdf4874b0b2ed1a048c5fcc2987
  • Redistribution grant: third_party/cpm22/LICENSE.txt, including the clarified 2022 grant for use, redistribution and modification of CP/M and derivatives.
  • The original source includes CP/M V2.2 BDOS Patch 01. The Python translator makes 8080-to-Z80 syntax changes for sjasmplus and places CCP at D000 and BDOS at D800. Its generated files stay in build/. Sources are the build authority.

TSRun local checkout: ../../TSRun, base commit 3cfca24b184ebe6106f8e88f9edd94a660dfc68d. Local modifications may exist; the tested source hashes, rather than the base commit alone, identify the core:

dock.js:parseDck confirms page types and payload sizes. machine.js:memRead, memWrite, and applyDckBlock implement writable DOCK/EXROM mapping. The test imports this actual machine and boots the supplied original ROMs from reset.

The community executable is installed under C:/apps/emulation/Fuse-JLODiskEmulation/fuse-1.9.1-win32. Its Oliger controller is not used by this image. Its exact source revision is not established by a binary version; do not infer one. Fuse/libspectrum upstream behavior from the handoff has now also been exercised by actual cartridge boots.

Build assembler: sjasmplus 1.20.3; Node test runtime: 24.19.0; Python: 3.10.6. The extracted assembler directory retains its upstream license. Fonts are derived from the user's ROM and generated artifacts should not be redistributed as a clean-room font implementation. No third-party ROM binaries are vendored.

The preloaded utilities now come from the pinned Digital Research distribution archive in third_party/cpm22-utilities.zip. See third_party/CPM_UTILITIES_NOTICE.md for its source, hash and redistribution basis. Utilities are vendored binaries; the CCP, BDOS, BIOS and cartridge loader continue to build from source.
